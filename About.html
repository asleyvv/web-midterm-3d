About TTG Marketplace

A friendly place for tabletop players to find, sell and trade their games.

Our mission

Good games should not sit on a shelf. TTG Marketplace helps players pass games on to people who will play them, and helps newcomers find affordable games to start with.

We care about three things:

  • Fair prices for buyers and sellers
  • Honest descriptions of each game's condition
  • A welcoming community for every kind of player

Frequently asked questions

Is it free to list a game?

Yes. Listing is free in this prototype. A small fee on completed sales could be added later.

Can I trade instead of selling?

Yes. When you create a listing you can mark it as open to trades and say which games you want in return.

How do I know a game's condition?

Every listing uses the same condition grades (New, Like new, Good, Fair) and includes photos. See the condition guide on the Browse page.
