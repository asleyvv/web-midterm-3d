THE HOUSE RULES

Good company.
Fair play.

A few small things make a better table for everyone.

  1. Make people feel welcome. Explain the rules and give new players time to learn.
  2. Play fairly. Respect other players, their choices, and their games.
  3. Trade honestly. Describe the condition and mention missing pieces before agreeing to a trade.
  4. Leave a tidy table. Count the components and put everything back after a game.
